Admissions

New students can apply by following these simple steps:

  1. Choose your preferred course.
  2. Collect and complete the application form.
  3. Submit your school certificates and other required documents.
  4. Visit the admissions office for help with the next steps.

Please contact the college to ask about current dates, fees, and required documents.
